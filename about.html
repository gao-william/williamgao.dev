Background

I’m William Gao, a cybersecurity major building practical experience through coursework, projects, and hands-on labs. I created this site to document that work clearly and to show how I approach technical problems.

I’m especially interested in understanding how systems and networks behave under normal and malicious conditions. My work has included malware analysis, Windows domain administration, network services, vulnerability assessment, and security tooling written in C and Python.
